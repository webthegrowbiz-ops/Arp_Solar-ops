SOLAR PLANNING

The details make the difference.

A practical rooftop solar discussion should consider more than the number of panels. We encourage customers to ask about the complete system and how it fits their property.

  • Electricity consumption and bill patterns
  • Usable roof area, condition and shade
  • On-grid, hybrid or off-grid suitability
  • Components, warranties and expected upkeep
  • Relevant DISCOM rules and current incentives
