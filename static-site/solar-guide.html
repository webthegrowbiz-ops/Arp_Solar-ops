THE SOLAR GUIDE

Your Guide to Rooftop Solar

A short guide to understanding the key decisions before you invest in rooftop solar in Delhi.

1. Start with your electricity bills

Collect a few recent electricity bills to understand average usage, seasonal variation and your electricity tariff. These help inform initial system sizing discussions.

2. Check your rooftop

Available area, shading, roof condition, orientation and safe access matter. A site assessment helps determine whether rooftop solar is practical.

3. Learn the types of systems

  • On-grid: generally operates together with the utility grid, subject to applicable rules and configuration.
  • Hybrid: may combine solar generation, batteries and grid electricity depending on system design.
  • Off-grid: typically relies on batteries or other storage and is more specialised.

4. Ask about permissions and incentives

Local electricity distribution company rules, net metering and government-scheme eligibility may change. Seek up-to-date information for your installation and the specific scheme before committing.

5. Review the proposed system

Ask for proposed capacity, equipment details, roof layout, indicative energy generation, warranties, after-sales support and clear pricing. Any projected savings should include assumptions.

6. Plan for ongoing care

Like other equipment, solar installations need periodic visual checks and appropriate maintenance. Follow safety instructions and use a qualified professional for electrical work.
