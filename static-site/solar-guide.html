LEARN ABOUT SOLAR

A practical guide to rooftop solar.

Explore the questions to ask before investing in a solar energy system for your property in Delhi.

Solar can be a valuable long-term investment, but actual cost, output and suitability depend on your property and local regulations. Use this checklist as a starting point—not as a site-specific quotation.

01. Understand your electricity usage

Collect several recent power bills, note the unit consumption and account for seasonal changes. These figures provide a starting point for estimating the right solar capacity.

02. Check your rooftop

Look at the available shade-free space, roof condition, access, orientation and any future construction plans. A professional assessment helps determine what is suitable and safe.

03. Compare system types

On-grid

Connected to the utility grid, with export and settlement governed by applicable local rules.

Hybrid

May use batteries as well as the grid, depending on the equipment and configuration.

Off-grid

Typically requires energy storage and careful planning for when sunlight is limited.

04. Check permissions and available schemes

Net metering and any subsidy eligibility depend on current government and DISCOM requirements. Verify terms through official sources before making financial decisions.

05. Compare detailed proposals

Ask for the proposed capacity, layout, panel and inverter specifications, installation inclusions, maintenance guidance, equipment warranties and any assumptions behind projected energy savings.

06. Plan for long-term care

Regular inspections, cleaning when appropriate and qualified electrical maintenance help keep the system operating as intended. Follow manufacturer and installer safety advice.

Important to know: Any illustrations or savings examples are educational only. Roof conditions, electricity tariffs, policies and sunlight affect the final recommendation and results.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A little clarity goes
a long way.

New to rooftop solar? Start with these frequently asked questions.

01Is my rooftop suitable for solar?+

It depends on the roof area, shade, orientation, condition and your electricity usage. Share the property details to discuss the next steps.

02Will solar reduce my electricity bill?+

A properly sized rooftop solar system can reduce grid electricity use. Your actual savings depend on usage, sunlight, equipment, applicable tariffs and local policies.

03Do government subsidies apply?+

Subsidy and net-metering eligibility depend on current government and DISCOM rules, as well as the property and installation. Confirm the latest eligibility before making a decision.

04Which areas do you cover?+

ARP Solar Energy is based in Delhi. Please contact the team to confirm service availability for your exact Delhi NCR location.

05How much space do I need for solar?+

Space needs depend on the desired system size, solar-panel dimensions, access pathways and available shade-free area. A site assessment will help determine what fits.

06How long does installation take?+

Project timelines vary depending on roof readiness, system design, approvals, equipment and site conditions. Ask for a project-specific schedule.
