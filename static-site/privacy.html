Privacy Policy

How information works on this ARP Solar Energy first-draft website.

Overview

This website provides information about solar solutions and offers a way to prepare an enquiry. The initial version does not include a website-hosted customer database or automatic contact-form submission.

Enquiries by email

When you select “Prepare my enquiry,” your device may open its configured email application with a draft addressed to ARP Solar Energy. The enquiry is not sent unless you choose to send it from that application. Any information sent by email may be used to respond to your question about solar services.

Information you choose to provide

An enquiry may include your name, email address, property location, property type and solar requirements. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in your enquiry.

Website technology

This draft does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run an analytics tracker. The hosting provider may process limited technical request data, such as IP address and access logs, for security and site delivery.

Third-party links and imagery

Some links may open a separate service or your email application. Illustrative photography shown on this draft is not a record of completed ARP Solar Energy projects.

Questions or corrections

For privacy questions, contact arpsolarenergy3@gmail.com. This contact address should be confirmed by ARP Solar Energy before launch.

This policy reflects the website's current first-draft configuration and must be updated if analytics, forms, hosting or other data-processing features are added.
